 Welcome to my website! My name is Jiaqi Shi (施家奇) and I am a fifth-year PhD student in the Department of IEOR at Columbia University. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. <a href="http://www.columbia.edu/~ae2516/">Adam Elmachtoub</a>. Previously, I received a B.S. in Mathematics from Nanjing University, followed by an M.S. in Operations Research from Columbia University.

 My research focuses on revenue management and pricing. I combine tools from queueing theory and optimization to develop simple, implementable policies with strong performance guarantees, with applications to reusable resources, vehicle sharing networks, and opaque products. I am also interested in pricing and operational decisions in markets shaped by AI.
